## Brief

A payment processor was losing ₹2 crore monthly to synthetic identity fraud. Rule-based systems caught obvious patterns but missed sophisticated fraud rings that rotated identities and merchants.

## Build

We modeled the transaction network as a graph, with users, merchants, and devices as nodes. PyTorch Geometric detects anomalous subgraphs in real-time. Kafka streams transactions, and Cassandra stores graph snapshots for model retraining.

## Result

Fraud detection precision improved from 54% to 82%, reducing false positives by half. Monthly fraud losses dropped 63%. The system now monitors 8 million transactions daily across 40,000 merchants.
